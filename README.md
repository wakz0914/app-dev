# Baki 🥊

**Baki** is my favorite martial-arts anime and manga series by [Keisuke Itagaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grappler_Baki).

> *"You cannot win against the world's strongest creature."* — Yujiro Hanma

## Why I love it

1. Over-the-top martial-arts choreography
2. **Yujiro Hanma**, the strongest creature on Earth
3. *Literally* impossible training arcs that still hit hard

## Main fighters

| Fighter | Style | Threat level |
|:--------|:------|-------------:|
| Baki Hanma | Mixed martial arts | 🥇 |
| Yujiro Hanma | All styles | 💀 |
| Doppo Orochi | Karate | 🥋 |
